# Architecture and Engineering Decision Log
## Team: T096 · Problem Statement: PS-027

This decision log documents key architectural, numerical, and business logic choices made during the development of the Merchant Cash Advance & Revenue-Based Financing Engine.

| Entry ID | Date | Context / Dilemma | Options Considered | Decision Made | Rationale / Viva Defense |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DEC-01** | 2026-10-18 | Language selection for BFSI engine. | Python 3.10+ vs. Java 17+. | **Python 3.10+** | Rapid prototyping, built-in statistical libraries, clean dataclass modeling, and ease of live code modification during the 15-minute viva. |
| **DEC-02** | 2026-10-20 | Architecture pattern for command-file simulation. | Microservices vs. Monolithic script vs. Clean Modular OOP. | **Clean Modular OOP** | Decoupled domain models, underwriting logic, simulation state, and reporting for 100% unit-testability without UI or framework overhead. |
| **DEC-03** | 2026-10-22 | Floating-point precision for currency sweeps. | Native float vs. Decimal vs. Integer paisa. | **Floored integer rupee (`math.floor`) with 2-decimal money fields** | Conforms exactly to BR-06 specification ("rounded down to rupee") while matching standard banking ledger conventions. |
| **DEC-04** | 2026-10-23 | Calculation of Coefficient of Variation (CV). | Sample standard deviation ($N-1$) vs. Population standard deviation ($N$). | **Population Standard Deviation (`statistics.pstdev`)** | The 6 calendar months represent the entire underwriting reference population, not an inferential sample. |
| **DEC-05** | 2026-10-24 | Handling of CV risk band boundaries (0.15 and 0.45). | Open intervals $(a,b)$ vs. Closed upper intervals $(a, b]$. | **Closed upper intervals (`CV <= 0.15`, `CV <= 0.45`)** | Ensures boundary cases like MCH-001 (CV=0.1500) and MCH-008 (CV=0.4500) qualify for expected factor bands (1.12 and 1.25). |
| **DEC-06** | 2026-10-25 | Holdback adjustment when expected term is exactly 270 days. | Trigger 1-point step up vs. Keep default 12%. | **Retain default 12% holdback** | 270 days is within the valid range $[120, 270]$ days; adjustments only trigger when strictly outside. |
| **DEC-07** | 2026-10-27 | Net settlement calculation when refunds exceed sales. | Propagate negative balance vs. Clamp sweep to Rs 0. | **Clamp daily sweep to Rs 0.00** | Lenders cannot pull negative funds from payment aggregator settlements; negative days yield zero sweep. |
| **DEC-08** | 2026-10-28 | Feed row deduplication strategy for aggregator feeds. | Overwrite existing records vs. Ignore subsequent duplicate rows. | **Ignore subsequent duplicate rows keyed on `(merchant_id, date)`** | Prevents double-sweeping on duplicate feed transmissions (e.g. MCH-001 on 2026-08-12). |
| **DEC-09** | 2026-10-29 | Handling out-of-order settlement feed records. | Stream processing by file order vs. Date-indexed daily batches. | **Sort and index events chronologically by calendar date** | Protects simulation state from file transposition errors (e.g. MCH-002 on 2026-09-10 appearing out of sequence). |
| **DEC-10** | 2026-10-31 | Precedence ordering between Daily Sweeps and True-Ups. | True-up first vs. Sweep first. | **Sweep first, then True-Up** | True-up must evaluate collections including the current day's business settlement prior to adjusting the forward holdback. |
| **DEC-11** | 2026-11-01 | Interpretation of True-up boundary condition at 130%. | Inclusive ($\ge 130\%$) vs. Strict inequality ($> 130\%$). | **Strict inequality ($> 130\%$)** | Brief specifies "collections are > 130% of plan"; exact 130.00% (MCH-003) must not trigger holdback reduction. |
| **DEC-12** | 2026-11-02 | Effective timing of True-Up holdback adjustments. | Immediate same-day sweep vs. Effective from next day ($n+1$). | **Effective from next day ($n+1$)** | Specification dictates "from the next day"; current day sweeps at holdback in force during morning job run. |
| **DEC-13** | 2026-11-03 | Bank mandate debit presentation on bank holiday Mondays. | Suppress mandate debit vs. Present and record failure. | **Present debit and record failure** | Banking NACH presentations are automated; failures (e.g. MCH-006 on Ganesh Chaturthi) apply charge and increment missed weeks. |
| **DEC-14** | 2026-11-04 | Early Payoff discount tier boundary on Day 90. | 50% tier vs. 25% tier. | **25% discount tier on Day 90** | Specification defines 50% discount "before day 90" ($\text{Day} < 90$); Day 90 falls into the subsequent 25% tier. |
| **DEC-15** | 2026-11-05 | Handling of post-DEFAULT settlement events. | Continue holdback sweeps vs. Cease sweeps in favor of recovery demand. | **Cease all daily sweeps immediately upon DEFAULT** | Under BR-10, DEFAULT accelerates remaining debt plus 5% penalty and revokes voluntary aggregator sweeps in favor of legal recovery. |
