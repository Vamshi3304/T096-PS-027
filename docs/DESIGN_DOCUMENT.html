1. Executive Summary & Domain Glossary

1.1 Problem Overview

Traditional commercial banking relies on fixed monthly installments (EMIs) with compounding interest, which frequently penalizes small merchants during seasonal sales dips. Revenue-Based Financing (RBF) and Merchant Cash Advances (MCA) solve this working-capital challenge by purchasing a specified amount of future payment receivables at an agreed purchase price.

Instead of charging interest:

  • The lender advances upfront capital (Advance).
  • The merchant agrees to repay a fixed total sum (Repayment Cap = Advance × Factor Rate).
  • The lender automatically sweeps a fixed percentage (Holdback %) of daily card and UPI settlement payouts.
  • If daily revenue is high, repayment accelerates; if revenue drops, repayment naturally slows down without default fees.
  • Periodic True-Ups dynamically adjust the holdback percentage mid-term to maintain expected tenure, while bank mandates protect against severe underperformance.

1.2 Domain Glossary

Term Industry & Technical Definition
Gross Sales Total customer payments processed via Point-of-Sale (POS) card swipes or UPI QR codes prior to aggregator fees.
MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) Processing fee charged by payment aggregators (typically ~1.5% of gross sales).
Refunds Reversals and refunds issued on customer returns. Deducted directly from daily settlement receipts.
Net Settlement Gross Sales − MDR − Refunds. The actual daily cash payout disbursed by the aggregator to the merchant, against which holdback sweeps are calculated.
Advance Amount Upfront capital funded to the merchant, sized as max 1.2× basis monthly settlements, capped at Rs 25,00,000.
Factor Rate Fixed pricing multiplier (1.12, 1.18, or 1.25) determined by cash-flow volatility (CV). MCA uses no compounding interest; the factor is the only financing cost.
Repayment Cap Total repayment obligation: Advance × Factor Rate.
Holdback Percentage Share (default 12%, range 8%–20%) withheld from each net settlement payout to amortize the balance.
Expected Term Estimated repayment duration in days: Cap / (Holdback × Average Daily Settlement). Statutory target is 120 to 270 days.
True-Up Periodic calibration on Day 60 and every 30 days after. If cumulative collections > 130% of plan, holdback drops 2 points; if < 70%, holdback rises 2 points.
Bank Mandate Pre-authorized e-NACH mandate on merchant's bank account to debit weekly shortfalls on Mondays when weekly sweeps fall below 50% of expectation.
WATCH Status Delinquency warning status triggered upon 2 missed weekly shortfall debits. Blocks all contract renewals.
DEFAULT Status Default status triggered upon 4 missed weekly shortfall debits. Accelerates total remaining balance plus 5% penalty, and ceases all future daily sweeps.
Early Payoff Settlement before maturity: discounts 50% of uncollected factor portion before Day 90, or 25% discount on or after Day 90.
Refinance Buyout of financing from an external lender upon presentation of a payoff letter. Sizing is capped at 1.0× monthly settlements.

6. Business Rule Register (BR-01 through BR-14)

Rule ID Rule Name Mathematical Formula / Criteria Output / State Impact
BR-01 Merchant Eligibility • Days of settlement history $\ge 180$
• Average monthly settlements $\ge$ Rs 1,00,000
• No month settlements < 40% of 6m average
• Business age $\ge 12$ full months 		eligible = True/False. If False, records failed rule IDs.
BR-02 Advance Amount Sizing $\text{Basis} = \min(\text{avg}_{\text{6m}}, \text{avg}_{\text{3m}})$
$\text{Advance} = \min(\text{requested}, 1.2 \times \text{Basis}, 2500000.00)$ 		Sized advance amount capped at Rs 25 Lakh.
BR-03 Factor Rate & Repayment Cap $\text{CV} = \sigma_{\text{pop}} / \mu_{\text{6m}}$
$\text{CV} \le 0.15 \implies 1.12$
$0.15 < \text{CV} \le 0.30 \implies 1.18$
$0.30 < \text{CV} \le 0.45 \implies 1.25$
$\text{CV} > 0.45 \implies \text{Ineligible}$
$\text{Cap} = \text{Advance} \times \text{Factor}$ 		Establishes total monetary repayment obligation.
BR-04 Holdback & Expected Term Default holdback = 12%.
$\text{Term} = \text{Cap} / (\text{Holdback} \times \text{avg}_{\text{daily}})$
Target range: $[120, 270]$ days.
Step holdback in 1% within $[8\%, 20\%]$. If still $>270$, reduce advance to fit 270 days. 		Sets holdback rate and expected loan tenure.
BR-05 Fee Structure, GST & APR $\text{Fee} = 0.02 \times \text{Advance}$
$\text{GST} = 0.18 \times \text{Fee}$
$\text{Disbursal} = \text{Advance} - \text{Fee} - \text{GST}$
$\text{APR} = \frac{\text{Cap}-\text{Adv}}{\text{Adv}} \times \frac{365}{\text{Term}} \times 100$ 		Net disbursed funds credited to merchant and APR disclosure.
BR-06 Daily Sweep $\text{Sweep} = \lfloor \text{Holdback} \times \text{Net Settlement} \rfloor$
Net $\le 0 \implies \text{Sweep} = 0$. Day 0 sweeps 0.
Sweep cannot exceed remaining balance. 		Daily cash collection credited to account.
BR-07 Job Precedence Sweep $\to$ True-Up $\to$ Weekly Minimum $\to$ Delinquency $\to$ Term Review Strict priority ordering of daily mutations.
BR-08 Mid-Term True-Up Evaluated on Day 60 and every 30 days after.
$\text{Plan}_n = (\text{Cap} \times n) / \text{Term}$
• If $\text{Cumulative} > 1.30 \times \text{Plan} \implies \text{HB} = \max(8\%, \text{HB}-2\%)$
• If $\text{Cumulative} < 0.70 \times \text{Plan} \implies \text{HB} = \min(20\%, \text{HB}+2\%)$
Effective from Day $n+1$. 		Adjusts daily holdback percentage mid-term.
BR-09 Weekly Minimum Shortfall Evaluated Mondays for preceding Mon–Sun (7 days).
$\text{Threshold} = 0.50 \times (\text{Cap}/\text{Term} \times 7)$
Shortfall debited from bank mandate. Failed debit adds Rs 500 charge + 1 missed week. 		Automated shortfall collection via bank mandate.
BR-10 Delinquency & Default • 2 missed weeks $\implies$ WATCH (blocks renewals)
• 4 missed weeks $\implies$ DEFAULT (accelerates balance + 5% penalty, sweeps cease) 		Enforces risk escalation and legal recovery.
BR-11 Term Tracking If $\text{Day} \ge \text{Term} + 60 \implies \text{Flag: TERM\_REVIEW}$ Flags slow-paying merchants for portfolio review.
BR-12 Early Payoff Discount $\text{Factor Rem} = (\text{Cap}-\text{Adv}) - \frac{\text{Col}}{\text{Cap}}(\text{Cap}-\text{Adv})$
• Day < 90: 50% discount on factor remaining
• Day $\ge$ 90: 25% discount on factor remaining
$\text{Amount Due} = \text{Balance} - \text{Discount}$ 		Early contract payoff at discounted factor markup.
BR-13 Advance Renewal Allowed when $\text{Collected}/\text{Cap} \ge 60\%$ and status $\ne \text{WATCH}/\text{DEFAULT}$.
Nets off old remaining balance at zero discount. 		Closes old advance as RENEWED; opens new advance.
BR-14 External Refinance Requires payoff letter; advance capped at $1.0\times$ basis monthly settlements. Pays off other lender out of disbursal. Lender buyout net disbursal.

7. Resolution of Mandatory Edge Cases & Planted Traps

Edge Case Scenario Engine Resolution & Mathematical Handling
CV exactly 0.1500 & 0.4500 (MCH-001 & MCH-008) Upper boundaries are inclusive: CV <= 0.15 qualifies for 1.12; CV <= 0.45 qualifies for 1.25. MCH-001 receives factor 1.12; MCH-008 receives factor 1.25.
Expected Term exactly 270.00 Days (MCH-002) Term is exactly 270.00 days before holdback adjustment. Because $270.00 \le 270.00$ falls within the statutory $[120, 270]$ range, holdback remains at default 12%.
Week with Rs 0 settlements on all days (MCH-003) Calendar week 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-11 has zero settlements. Flagged ZERO_WEEK. Monday shortfall presented is the full 50% threshold (Rs 6,300.00).
Day 60 True-Up at exactly 130% of Plan (MCH-003) Plan = Rs 1,08,000.00; Actual Collections = Rs 1,40,400.00 (exactly 130.00%). Because rule specifies strictly > 130%, holdback is not reduced.
Early Payoff on Day 90 (MCH-004) Disbursal is Day 0; date 2026-09-29 is Day 90. Falls into the 25% discount tier ($\text{Day} \ge 90$). Discount is 25% of uncollected factor portion.
Renewal at exactly 60% of Cap (MCH-005) Cumulative collections after Day 135 sweep = Rs 2,90,304.00 = exactly $60.00\%$ of cap Rs 4,83,840.00. Because rule specifies $\ge 60\%$, renewal is accepted.
Monday Holiday Mandate Failure (MCH-006) 2026-09-14 is Ganesh Chaturthi bank holiday. Command MANDATE_FAIL registers failure. Engine records Rs 500 charge and increments missed weeks.
Negative Net Settlement (MCH-006) Gross sales Rs 4,200.00 − MDR Rs 63.00 − Refunds Rs 9,800.00 = Net −Rs 5,663.00. Sweep is safely clamped to Rs 0.00.
Ineligible Traps (MCH-009 & MCH-010) MCH-009 fails 40% monthly floor (March was Rs 60k < Rs 64k threshold). MCH-010 business age is 11 months (<12 months). Subsequent ACCEPT commands are rejected.
Duplicate & Out-of-Order Feeds Deduplication on (merchant_id, date) ensures duplicate rows (MCH-001 on 2026-08-12) are swept once. Feed events are sorted chronologically by date.
